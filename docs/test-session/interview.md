# Rob interview (after the photo shoot, about 10 minutes)

Questions as sent to Rob (2026-10-12). Film on iPhone in landscape, Rob in samue, seated in the zendo, light from the front. One question at a time, 30–60 seconds per answer.

1. How did you first come to Zen, and how long have you been practising?
2. How is zazen different from other kinds of meditation?
3. Who is your teacher, or how did you learn? Which tradition or lineage do you practise in?
4. How long have you been teaching zazen?
5. Why is Zen important, and why should people try it?
6. One message for someone who is thinking about trying zazen.
7. I've never meditated, and I'm not sure I can sit on the floor. Is this for me?

## How the answers are used
- 1, 3, 4: Rob's profile on the website and booking sites
- 2, 5: product description (what makes this different)
- 6: short clip for the top of the website
- 7: FAQ and booking-site "Know before you go"
