# Rob interview (after the photo shoot, about 10 minutes)

Film on iPhone in landscape, Rob seated in the zendo, light from the front. Ask one question at a time and let Rob answer in 30–60 seconds. Short answers are easier to cut into clips.

## About Rob (for the website and booking-site profile)
1. How did you first come to Zen, and how long have you been practising?
2. Who is your teacher, and which tradition or lineage do you practise in?
3. How long have you been teaching zazen, and who do you usually teach?

## About the session (clips for the website and listings)
4. What happens in a session with you, from the moment guests arrive?
5. I've never meditated. Is this for me?
6. What if I can't sit cross-legged on the floor?
7. Is zazen religious? Do I need to believe anything?
8. What do people usually feel after their first sitting?
9. Why learn zazen in Japan, in a real dojo, rather than from an app?

## One-liners (for the top of the page)
10. In one sentence: what is zazen?
11. In one sentence: why should a visitor to Tokyo try it?
