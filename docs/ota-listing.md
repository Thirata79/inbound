Tokyo: Zen Meditation in a Small Group with an English-Speaking Teacher

（Viatorで短くする場合：Tokyo Zazen: Zen Meditation Class in English or French）

Learn zazen, the seated meditation of Zen, in a quiet Tokyo dojo. A teacher guides you directly in English or French, in a group of no more than eight.

**Highlights（ハイライト）**
- Learn zazen step by step in a real Zen dojo in Tokyo
- Guided directly in English or French, with no interpreter in between
- Small group of up to 8 people, so the teacher can help each person
- Two sittings, walking meditation and time for your questions

**Full description（詳しい説明）**
Step away from Tokyo's crowds and sit in silence in a traditional Zen dojo in Koto City, a short walk from Kiyosumi-shirakawa and Monzen-nakacho stations.

Your teacher, Rob Daoust, practises and teaches zazen in Tokyo, and guides every session himself, in English or in French, his first language. There is no interpreter between you, so you can ask anything and understand everything.

The session begins with an introduction to how to sit, how to breathe and what zazen is. You then sit for a first period of meditation, practise kinhin (slow walking meditation), and sit a second time now that you know the way. The session ends with time for questions about Zen, meditation, or keeping a practice at home.

Groups are kept to eight people or fewer, so the teacher can look at your posture and help you find a way of sitting that works for you. No experience or religious background is needed.

**Includes（含まれるもの）**
- 75-minute zazen session with an English- or French-speaking teacher

- Small group of up to 8 people

**Not included（含まれないもの）**

- Wear loose, comfortable clothing you can sit cross-legged in. Avoid short skirts and shorts.

- Please arrive 10 minutes before the start time. Late arrivals may not be able to join once the meditation has begun.（何分前かはRobertの回答で直す）

- This is a practice session, not a religious ceremony. No belief or religious background is required.

- Aucune expérience nécessaire

**Description**
Loin de l'agitation de Tokyo, asseyez-vous en silence dans un dojo zen traditionnel de l'arrondissement de Koto, à quelques minutes à pied des stations Kiyosumi-shirakawa et Monzen-nakacho.

Votre enseignant, Rob Daoust, originaire du Québec, pratique et enseigne le zazen à Tokyo. Il guide chaque séance lui-même, en français ou en anglais.

La séance commence par une introduction : comment s'asseoir, comment respirer, ce qu'est le zazen. Vous vous asseyez ensuite une première fois, pratiquez le kinhin (marche méditative lente), puis vous vous asseyez une seconde fois. La séance se termine par un temps de questions sur le zen, la méditation ou la pratique à la maison.

Aucune expérience ni croyance religieuse n'est nécessaire.
