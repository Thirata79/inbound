Zen meditation (zazen) class in a small group, taught in English or French, in a traditional Zen dojo in Tokyo.

WHAT THIS IS
A 75-minute introduction to zazen, the seated meditation of Zen, for travelers with no experience. The session takes place in a quiet, working Zen dojo (meditation hall) with tatami floors, meditation cushions, a hanging scroll and bronze statues. The teacher, Rob Daoust, practises and teaches zazen in Tokyo and guides every session himself, directly in English or in French (his first language; he is from Quebec, Canada). There is no interpreter in between, so guests can ask anything and understand everything. Groups are limited to 6 people so the teacher can help each person with posture and breathing.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
1. Introduction: how to sit, how to breathe, and what zazen is.
2. First sitting: a first period of seated meditation on a cushion.
3. Walking meditation (kinhin): slow, mindful walking between sittings.
4. Second sitting: a second period of zazen, now that you know the way.
5. Questions: time to ask anything about Zen, meditation, or keeping a practice at home.

LOCATION
Koto City, Tokyo (Fukagawa area), a short walk from Kiyosumi-shirakawa Station and Monzen-nakacho Station. This is a quiet, traditional neighborhood near Kiyosumi Garden. The session takes place indoors, in the meditation hall of the dojo. Guests meet the teacher at the entrance of the dojo.
